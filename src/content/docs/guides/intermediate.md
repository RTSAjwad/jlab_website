## Super summary

- Is something unclear? [Contact me](/contact/) ("?" to the right).
- We'll use Anki and movie flashcards to step through the plot of a movie in
  bite-size chunks.
- Learning is most efficient when you focus on flashcards with easy sentences.
- Everything that's too difficult gets deleted in order to make learning fast.
- The Jlab Anki addon helps to quickly resolve unknown things.
- Repeating sentences with flashcards for a few days will drastically improve
  your skills.
- Reading skills are not required.

## Goal & requirements

Once you're familiar with the basics of Japanese, you can seriously start to
study with movies. This will teach you as many words as you like and ultimately
leads to perfect, fluent listening comprehension and good reading skills. In
order to start, you need to learn the basics of Japanese grammar and should know
around 1000 words. Being able to read Japanese is not required — Jlab teaches
this along the way.

## How to study with movies?

Bad news first: You won't learn much from just watching movies, at least not
until you reach upper intermediate / advanced level. Instead, we'll use movie
flashcards, which will lead you through the plot step by step.

You can repeat what's being said as often as you like for leveling up your
listening comprehension. Jlab's tools help you quickly figure out unknown
things. If you remember a card, Anki will show it again at increasing time
intervals, which will boost your skills. Afterwards, you'll be able to watch the
movie without subtitles.

## Let's go!

1. Download and install [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) — it will manage your
   flashcards.
2. Install the [Jlab Anki addon](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/2110939339)
   (Anki → Tools → Addons → Get Addons, then paste the ID: `2110939339`).
3. Get a movie flashcard deck you like [here](/decks/).
4. Import the deck into Anki and start with "listening comprehension".
5. Select text on your flashcards to resolve unknown words.
6. For more features, read [the addon's manual](/addon/manual/).

## Concentrate on all the easy parts

Easily comprehensible input will boost your learning speed, that's why you should
only focus on easy sentences. While movies are made for entertaining native
speakers, they also contain lots of easy parts — that's what we will dig out.
Everything else will be deleted. Simply read the English translation if
available, then press `Ctrl + Del` in Anki.

Here's what you want to **delete**:

- Boring parts
- Difficult sentences
- Things you're not interested in
- Cards that contain more than 1 unknown word or term
- Cards you understand immediately

Here's what to **keep**:

- _Only_ things you're interested in
- Cards with _at most_ 1 unknown term
- Cards without unknown words that you don't understand by listening
  comprehension immediately

## How to practice reading

Jlab splits learning into two parts: First, you resolve unknown words during
listening comprehension. Text on these flashcards should be displayed in
roumaji, this way you're not slowed down by the difficult Japanese writing
systems. After a while, listening cards turn into reading cards. Since you
already looked up all unknown words, you're guaranteed to understand the
sentences on these cards.

Using Jlab's kana and kanji trainer you can step by step mix in Japanese
characters and practice reading them in an intuitive way. It's also possible to
have all cards display in kana or kanji right away, if you prefer this.

## Be prepared to hit the wall

Studying with movies can be frustrating, because it will remind you of what
you're not yet able to understand every day. It is extremely important to get
used to — and ultimately to ignore — this. Deleting difficult flashcards right
away is absolutely essential here.

## Delete your flashcards to keep learning fun

It can't be stressed enough how important it is to delete your flashcards, in
particular boring and difficult parts. Don't ever be afraid of this! Ultimately,
even the easier cards must be deleted to make room for lots of new content — the
Jlab addon automatically takes care of this.

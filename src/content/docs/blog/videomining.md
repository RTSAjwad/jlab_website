After mostly finishing the beginner's course, work started on new decks for
intermediate learners. These are fully built around **video recommendations**. I
search for easily comprehensible videos on YouTube that fit your current level of
Japanese. Then I teach the most important unknown words to make watching the
videos as easy as possible — usually using more than just a single sentence.

These decks and the playlist with all videos are available for patrons only.
Note that the videos are all publicly available, so you can try finding more of
them yourself: check out the channels of the creators!

## How to watch the videos

I recommend the **Language Reactor** addon. It turns YouTube into a stop-and-go
player, has a dictionary and sometimes translations. The most important controls:

- **q**: toggle autoplay (on by default; try turning it off in the beginning)
- **a**: previous subtitle line
- **s**: replay current subtitle line
- **d**: next subtitle line

I also recommend re-watching the videos after a few days, but don't overdo it —
rewatching once or twice should be sufficient. To do so, just keep the flashcard
with the links in Anki until it shows up again.

Furigana characters are printed on top of kanji characters. They are a common
tool for making Japanese easier to read, because kanji can be read differently
depending on the context. A common question beginners often ask is: **How should
I learn all these kanji readings?**

In short, it's not recommended to learn these readings individually. A long
answer is written down in Tae Kim's blog. For those not yet familiar with how the
Japanese writing systems work, check out the related video.

---
title: How to Use the Intermediate Deck
description: Word sessions vs. video sessions, burying and deleting video cards, and handling video recommendations.
---

Compared to the beginner's course, the intermediate deck is different:

- It is built around video recommendations and prepares you to watch stuff on
  YouTube and anime episodes.
- It has **word booster cards**, which repeat a new word with different
  sentences to quickly improve your listening comprehension.
- It is managed by the Jlab addon, so you can automatically get rid of old cards
  (optional).
- Booster cards and video recommendations are arranged so you first see 2-3
  sample sentences with a new word a few times, then see the video.

## Word session vs. video session

Split learning into **word sessions** and **video sessions** instead of mixing
them. In a word session, learn new words by means of sentence cards, just as in
the beginner deck. In a video session, try to understand the videos on a video
card without doing new sentences.

This is optional — you can of course also do the video cards immediately.

## Bury video cards

While learning new words with sentence cards, a video card may show up. For
dedicated practice with the videos, **bury** the card for now. This removes it
from today's session so you can finish the new sentence cards first. On the next
day, the buried video card is the first one that shows up in your new cards.

**Don't mix "bury" with "suspend"!** Suspending a card deactivates it
permanently, burying just holds it back.

## Delete video cards

Do not keep video cards in Anki too long. They are meant to be deleted after you
understand the videos, not reviewed often. Maybe rewatch the videos after a few
days — but instead of keeping the card, have a look at the Jlab playlist linked
on Patreon; all videos are in there.

## Finish old cards first

Just as for the beginner's course, finish all old reviews before doing new cards.
In contrast to the beginner's course, the new deck is managed by the Jlab addon,
so you have the option to suspend very old cards automatically.

## 10 cards per session

Do 10 new cards / day. If you want to do more, don't do 20 at once — do 10 per
session. The flow is always this:

1. Do all pending reviews first (choose "Show new cards after reviews" in Tools
   → Preferences → Scheduling).
2. Set number of new cards to 10 (or less) in the deck options.
3. Do all the new cards, but don't shift them forward too fast if something is
   new. First, choose "hard" for all cards. Then, after some time on the same
   day, check them again and choose "good" for all cards you remember.
4. When all new cards are gone (moved forward using "good" or "easy") and you
   want more, adjust the amount of new cards in the deck options.

## How to handle individual video cards

### Video card shows up

- If in a sentence/word session, bury the card. It will show up the next day as
  the first new card. This is optional.
- Read the hints on the card, especially if not using the Language Reactor addon
  to watch the videos.
- Watch the video and try to understand what's being said.

### Language Reactor

Especially in the beginning, use the **Language Reactor** addon. It turns YouTube
into a stop-and-go player, has a dictionary and sometimes translations. The most
important controls:

- **q**: toggle autoplay (on by default; try turning it off in the beginning)
- **a**: previous subtitle line
- **s**: replay current subtitle line
- **d**: next subtitle line

Note that the subtitle quality on YouTube is often bad.

### Don't understand? No problem!

It is usually not necessary to understand every single word or sentence. The
important words are taught in advance, but not all words. Infrequent words or
those in unimportant parts are skipped.

If you do not understand every single word, this is fine! If you understand the
general message, you're fine. Keep in mind that listening comprehension is
difficult — don't let difficult content drag you down.

Hi, I'm **Joe**, creator of Japanese Like a Breeze (Jlab). I started to learn
Japanese for fun in my free time in 2015 and probably am on the same level as
you: still struggling, but having lots of fun! I love to study with movies and
other Japanese media and made it my mission to make this way of studying as easy
as possible. I hope you'll enjoy it as much as I do!

My studying habits may seem weird: I decided to shift learning kanji towards the
end of my journey. Instead, I focus on acquiring new words & sentences from
movies by listening comprehension with the help of the tools I'm developing. I'm
not in a hurry to become fluent and study only about 15-20 minutes a day on
average.

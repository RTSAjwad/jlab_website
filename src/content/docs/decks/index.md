Here's a list of all Anki decks created by Jlab. Most time is currently spent on
the intermediate decks. The beginner's course also gets updates. There are two
other decks in the long-term pipeline, not yet listed: one for learning kanji and
one for anime-specific language and pitfalls.

## Beginner's course, version 14

Starter deck with 2100 cards, available on
[Ankiweb](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/2110939339). This teaches most grammar
and around 1000 words. At 10 cards / day, it keeps you busy for 7 months.

## Vocabulary (patron only)

A supplementary deck that teaches ~500 words from the beginner's course in
isolation, available on [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/jlabjapanese).
If the information density of the beginner's course is too high, you can use it
to learn words in advance. Afterwards, the course should be a bit easier. The
audio was recorded by a Japanese voice actress.

## Numbers and Counting, version 1

Teaches numbers and how to count. You can download it from
[Ankiweb](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/2110939339). This is also linked in the
beginner's course and builds on its content.

## Intermediate 1, version 3 (patron only)

Follow-up of the beginner's course. Get the download link on
[Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/jlabjapanese). Fully built around
video recommendations, it helps you quickly understand videos on YouTube. It has
1000 cards and prepares you to watch 150 videos on YouTube and one episode of
anime.

## Intermediate 2, version 2 (patron only)

~900 new cards teach words required to understand ~110 YouTube videos. Video
recommendation highlights: Hitoki from Onomappu learns Spanish for a month then
tries to date girls; friendship, love hotels, kitanai kotoba, kids embarrassed
by the hairdresser, giving tips (gratuity) in Japan, moving a lot, uchi vs. ie
and more. Get the download link on
[Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/jlabjapanese).

## Intermediate 3, version 1 (patron only)

Work in progress, ~320 new cards prepare you to watch ~40 videos on YouTube.
Video recommendation highlights: First love (natsukashii desu ne 🙂), koto ha
nai explained (nihongo no mori), let's just be friends (voiced manga clip), ni
taishite explained, ni vs de to indicate a place, and a few other interesting
grammar topics. Get the download link on
[Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/jlabjapanese).

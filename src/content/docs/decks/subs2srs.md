Jlab maintains a large collection of community-created subs2srs decks. Those are
TV shows converted into flashcards and were one of the most valuable resources
of the entire study journey. All decks are available
[here](https://www.mediafire.com/folder/h3c999hbd9r6m/subs2srs_decks).

This collection was started by someone else who did not want to maintain it
anymore. Jlab took over the hosting afterwards to preserve it for everyone. The
folder is very unorganized — neither the previous owner nor Jlab took the time
to clean it up. Many decks are broken (e.g. have no image or wrong audio). There
is a list of decks used for mining that mostly work.
